      Using the icons

      • On a slide

        Download the SVG and drag it on to a slide, or use “Copy image” and paste. SVGs stay sharp at any size.

      • Recolour

        Single-colour SVGs take any colour with Graphics Fill. To recolour parts of a full-colour icon, right-click it and choose Convert to Shape.

      • Posters and print

        Use the SVG, or a 2048–4096 px PNG. Figures come with or without labels, so you can add your own.
